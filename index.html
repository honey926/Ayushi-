A LITTLE SOMETHING I WANTED TO SAY

Ayushi ❤️

Sometimes the person we care about the most
is the person we end up hurting without meaning to.

I'm Sorry... 🥺

I know things haven't been perfect lately.

And I know that sometimes my overthinking makes me say things I don't actually mean.

I think too much, I react too quickly, and sometimes my emotions speak before I can think properly.

For that, I'm genuinely sorry.

I Know I Overthink... ❤️

Sometimes a small thing becomes a hundred thoughts inside my head.

I start imagining things, questioning things, and worrying about things that probably don't even deserve that much thought.

But I understand that my overthinking should never become a reason for you to feel hurt.

I am trying to understand myself better and become better at handling my emotions.

One Thing I Want You To Know...

I'm not writing this to make you feel guilty.

I'm not expecting everything to become normal just because I said sorry.

I just wanted to take responsibility for my part.

Because sometimes saying "I was wrong" is more important than trying to explain why we were wrong.

And this time, I just want to say:

I'm sorry for overthinking. I'm sorry for reacting. I'm sorry for hurting you.

Before You Go... ❤️

I don't know what you're thinking right now.

Maybe you're still upset. Maybe you need some time. And that's okay.

I just hope you know that this message isn't about making excuses.

It's simply me admitting that I could have handled things better.

No pressure. No expectations.

Just a genuine I'm sorry. ❤️

Ayushi,

If I could take back the moments where my overthinking caused you pain, I would.

But since I can't change the past, I can only learn from it.

So I'm going to try to listen more, think before reacting, and not let unnecessary thoughts turn into unnecessary fights.

I'm really sorry. ❤️

— Honey
